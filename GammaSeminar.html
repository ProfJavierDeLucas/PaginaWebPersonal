Gamma Seminar

Geometry and Applications: Modern Mathematical Approaches

International seminar in geometry and mathematical physics

About the seminar

The Gamma Seminar is a weekly international online seminar devoted to foundational and advanced topics in geometry and mathematical physics. It brings together researchers connected with the University of Warsaw, Universitat Rovira i Virgili and Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, and is open to participants from other institutions.

The seminar aims in particular to support advanced training of young researchers and graduate students while providing regular lectures by internationally recognised experts.

Main topics

  • Geometric mechanics and field theories.
  • Symplectic, Poisson and contact geometry and their generalisations.
  • Jet geometry, differential equations and geometric structures.
  • Integrable systems, superposition rules and Lie systems.
  • Supergeometry.
  • Stochastic and numerical geometric methods.
  • Orbifolds and related geometric structures.
