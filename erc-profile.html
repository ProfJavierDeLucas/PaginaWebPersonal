Geometric structures for differential equations and field theory

I develop geometric frameworks that organise differential equations, symmetries, reduction methods and field theories. The current central line is the use of k-contact geometry and jet geometry as a common language for PDEs, non-conservative field theories, Lie systems and geometric mechanics.

Research programme

The organising question is whether modern contact-type and jet-type geometries can provide a new structural language for differential equations, field theories, transformations, reductions and integrability phenomena.

Core message visible to reviewers

My recent research builds a coherent programme around k-contact geometry, jet bundles, Lie systems, Hamilton–Jacobi theory, and geometric reduction. The goal is not only to adapt known symplectic/contact methods to field theory, but to identify intrinsic geometric structures that naturally encode PDEs, non-conservative dynamics, superposition rules, symmetries and transformations.

Four research pillars

1. k-contact and field theories

Development of k-contact geometry as a framework for non-conservative field theories, reduction, Hamilton–Jacobi theory and dissipative PDE models.

2. Jets and PDE geometry

Recognition of jet bundles through higher-order polarised k-contact structures, with applications to symmetries, invariant submanifolds and transformations.

3. Lie systems and superposition

Geometric study of non-autonomous differential equations, compatible geometric structures, superposition rules and integrability mechanisms.

4. Reduction and geometric mechanics

Marsden–Weinstein-type reductions, energy-momentum methods, stability, multisymplectic and related geometric structures.

Group, training and environment

Gamma Research Group

The Gamma Research Group is an internationally oriented research environment at the University of Warsaw, focused on geometric methods in mathematics and mathematical physics. It combines research on jets, symmetries, differential equations, field theories, geometric mechanics, Lie systems and modern reduction methods.

  • Weekly online Gamma seminar.
  • Hybrid reading group on Lie groupoids and Lie algebroids.
  • Monographic courses and student research projects.
  • Collaborations with URV Tarragona, UPC Barcelona, CRM Montréal and other institutions.
