Javier de Lucas Araujo

Geometric structures for differential equations and field theory

I develop geometric frameworks for PDEs and field theories, with emphasis on symmetry, reduction, integrability and non-conservative dynamics. A central direction is the use of k-contact geometry and jet geometry as a unifying language.
